# Role Boundaries and Review Questions

## Product / lead design

Own positioning, pillars, audience, product scope, success criteria, cross-system decisions and final design approval. Do not let execution disciplines silently decide product tradeoffs.

## System design

Own inventory, resources, economy, loot, shops, builds, meta progression, settlement and persistence rules. Require sources/sinks, reset boundaries, seed behavior, safeguards, UI explanation and telemetry.

## Combat design

Own controls in combat, weapons/abilities, targeting, damage, defense, enemies, attacks, states, feedback and encounter strength. Require readable tells, counterplay, timing windows, authority boundaries and performance degradation rules.

## Level design

Own regions, topology, modules, routes, encounter/loot sockets, objective space, hubs, pacing and spatial budgets. Require reachability, retreat paths, spawn readability, connector compatibility, streaming/navigation checks and generation fallback.

## Narrative

Own premise, character/faction arcs, narrative delivery, terminology and content boundaries. Connect narrative beats to playable states and production budgets.

## UI/UX

Own information hierarchy, screens, HUD, interactions, settings and accessibility. UI presents state and submits intent; it does not own gameplay truth.

## Art, animation, VFX and audio

Own style systems, asset specifications, feedback layers, budgets and acceptance evidence. Each asset has purpose, owner, dependency, status, source/license and acceptance capture.

## Engineering

Own technical architecture, data/runtime boundaries, authority, persistence, random streams, tools, builds and measured performance. Engineering constraints must be reflected in design decisions, not stored only in implementation notes.

## Production

Own milestones, WBS, RACI/ownership, dependencies, capacity, sourcing, review cadence and exit criteria. Always distinguish internal ownership, joint work and outsourced delivery.

## QA / analytics

Own reproducible evidence, coverage, telemetry definitions, defect gates and regression. Acceptance criteria must be observable and repeatable; PIE/local mocks do not replace required target-platform or network tests.
